# Marinara CR Review

Read the [shared contract](../marinara-change-request/SHARED_CONTRACT.md), approved HLD/plan, changed-file scope, and validation handoff. Review independently and read-only: identify substantive defects, acceptance gaps, scope drift, or residual risks. Do not fix findings in this stage.

Exit with concise findings tied to evidence, or an explicit no-substantive-defect result. Route CodeRabbit observations through `$marinara-coderabbit-review`.
